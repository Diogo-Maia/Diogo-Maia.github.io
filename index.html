01 — About

Engineering for systems that have to keep running.

I'm a Lead Software Engineer focused on designing and developing .NET applications, backend systems and enterprise software. My professional experience spans telecommunications, public infrastructure, procurement, insurance and international organisations — working with both modern and legacy .NET ecosystems.

Day to day that means C#, .NET Core, ASP.NET, microservices, Domain-Driven Design, REST APIs and SQL databases, alongside modern delivery practices — containers, CI/CD and automated pipelines. My background also includes full-stack development with React and TypeScript, which helps when a backend decision has to survive contact with a real UI.

Throughout my career I've worked on large-scale, business-critical platforms: procurement and tendering systems, infrastructure management and telecommunications applications. I'm particularly interested in backend engineering, distributed systems, software architecture, system integration and building maintainable applications that can evolve over time.

  • Domain first

    Model the business before the framework. DDD keeps complex domains readable years later.

  • Integration by design

    Services rarely live alone. Clear contracts, async messaging and sane failure modes.

  • Delivery you can trust

    Automated builds, containerised workloads and pipelines that make releases boring.

  • Leading in the open

    Technical decisions explained, reviewed and documented — so the team can carry them.
